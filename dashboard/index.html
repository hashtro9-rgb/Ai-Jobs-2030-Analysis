AI's Impact on Jobs by 2030

Interactive exploration of 3,000 simulated workforce records across 20 roles, 10 industries, and 10 countries.

Read this first: formal hypothesis tests (ANOVA, chi-square, correlation) find no statistically significant relationship between AI replacement risk and industry, job title, salary, or experience in this dataset — the fields appear independently randomized. Explore the patterns below, but treat them as artifacts of a synthetic dataset, not real labor-market claims. Full write-up in the README.
